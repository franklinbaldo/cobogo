# Sisprev

Sisprev is a high-confidence consumer candidate because it already projects a living OKF legal-audit corpus into a human-facing Astro site and publishes review artifacts.

It is especially valuable as a bridge between Astronauta-like dense administration and CausaGanha-like public data reading: the surface must expose rules, validation state, evidence and legal reasoning without flattening them into generic cards or dashboard KPIs.

Candidate status does not imply Cobogó adoption.
