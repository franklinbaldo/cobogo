# `knowledge-policy`

A policy governing how evidence becomes, remains, or is rejected from canonical Cobogó knowledge.

## Current required frontmatter

- `status`
- `title`

## Current optional frontmatter

- `description`

## Boundary

Knowledge policy governs evidence and promotion. It must not be used to manufacture adoption, consumer state or factual authority that downstream evidence has not established.
