# `design-grammar`

A reusable visual relation derived from canon/reference evidence; it describes structure rather than a component implementation.

## Current required frontmatter

- `constrained_by`
- `relation`
- `title`

## Current optional frontmatter

- `description`

## Boundary

Grammar names a relation and its constraint. Framework, component API, palette and consumer-local identity remain downstream unless separately promoted by evidence.
