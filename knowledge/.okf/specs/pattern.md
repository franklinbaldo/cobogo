# `pattern`

An evidence-backed reusable relation shared across consumers; status controls maturity and must not imply a universal component.

## Current required frontmatter

- `consumer_evidence`
- `related_issue`
- `status`
- `title`

## Current optional frontmatter

- `description`

## Boundary

Consumer evidence must remain recoverable. A pattern generalizes the smallest shared semantic relation; framework, component, palette and local identity stay downstream unless independently justified.
