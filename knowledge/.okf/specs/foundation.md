# `foundation`

A semantic foundation that underpins shared Cobogó contracts without prescribing consumer identity.

## Current required frontmatter

- `related_issues`
- `status`
- `title`

## Current optional frontmatter

- `description`

## Boundary

Foundations express shared semantic authority. Concrete brand, density and product workflow remain consumer-local unless separately supported by cross-consumer evidence.
