# `operational-practice`

A reproducible operational method that affects the quality or interpretation of design evidence.

## Current required frontmatter

- `last_verified`
- `related_issues`
- `status`
- `title`

## Current optional frontmatter

- `description`

## Boundary

Operational practice may describe configured capability, execution, artifact, deploy or capture, but those evidence classes must remain distinguishable.
