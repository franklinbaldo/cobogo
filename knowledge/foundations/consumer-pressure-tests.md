# Consumer pressure tests for foundations

A foundation role is stronger when it survives materially different consumer contexts without forcing identical values or layouts.

## Public analytical/data reading

CausaGanha, Ficha, Baliza Site and Sites Prefeituras pressure `data`/`inscription` typography, compact-but-readable spacing, table/filter states, freshness/provenance, comparison and brand-vs-state color separation.

## Dense review/audit

Astronauta and Sisprev pressure compact density, visible focus/selection/editing, non-color status semantics, long reasoning text and formal/paginated output.

## Editorial/publication

O Vigia, Quem São Eles?, Franklin blog, The Theater, The Lab, Rationality Club LatAm and Adi blog pressure sustained-reading typography, chronology/archive, contextual provenance and very different expressive identities without one editorial skin.

## Authoring/preview

Intuit and Formato Legal pressure clear region ownership, source→preview relationships, keyboard focus, responsive pane switching and theme overrides that preserve semantics.

## Document/print

Formato Legal and Sisprev pressure screen→print projection, page rhythm, headings, metadata and tables without a second foundation ontology.

## Immersive/game

Aleph the Game pressures purposeful motion, narrative state and expressive interaction. This remains experimental evidence, not a stable game layer.

## Self-dogfood: Cobogó

Cobogó itself pressures the same public contract through documentation, reference/specimen surfaces and future knowledge navigation.

Self-dogfood fails if Cobogó needs a private token/API unavailable to consumers. It also fails epistemically if a Cobogó-local aesthetic preference is promoted to shared core merely because the reference site uses it.

## Cross-context checks

A candidate role fails if it requires one fixed density, couples brand color to status meaning, forces a component/runtime dependency for basic foundations, needs a parallel state vocabulary for large tables, or cannot map between passive reading, editing and print where applicable.

## Promotion rule

Prefer evidence across at least two distinct consumer contexts or a strong platform/accessibility invariant. Product-specific workflow, legal convention, game mechanic or brand need stays local unless repeated evidence justifies promotion.
