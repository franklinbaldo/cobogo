# Consumer pressure tests for foundations

A foundation role is stronger when it survives materially different consumer contexts without forcing identical values or layouts.

## Public analytical/data reading

### CausaGanha
Needs clear tables, status, source/freshness, comparison and judicial identifiers under a static HTML-first architecture.

Pressure:
- `data` and `inscription` typography roles;
- compact but readable `adjacent`/`group` spacing;
- distinct brand accent vs state colors;
- light/dark mode mapping;
- visible focus with low JS.

### Ficha
Needs company lookup, analytical filters and temporal snapshot reading over large datasets.

Pressure:
- table/filter density without card proliferation;
- snapshot freshness/provenance state;
- numeric/identifier comparison;
- loading/stale/error states.

### Baliza Site
Needs search, analytical result tables, downloads and browser-local SQL.

Pressure:
- result-table and filter semantics;
- export/download affordance states;
- analytical density distinct from CausaGanha branding.

### Sites Prefeituras
Needs comparison/ranking across 5,570 municipalities and many audit metrics.

Pressure:
- very large table/ranking readability;
- metric/state semantics that remain accessible at high density;
- strong filtering/orientation without dashboard ornament overload.

## Dense review/audit

### Astronauta
Needs high-density model/field/relation/status review and editing.

Pressure:
- compact spacing profile;
- very clear focus/selected/editing states;
- status semantics independent of brand;
- geometry/elevation that communicate edit/review layers rather than decorate.

### Sisprev
Needs rule-by-rule legal review, provenance, validation status, reasoning and paginated reports.

Pressure:
- text hierarchy strong enough for legal reasoning;
- metadata/state visible without overwhelming the rule body;
- audit/validation states with non-color redundancy;
- screen roles that survive formal paginated output.

## Editorial/publication

### O Vigia
Needs factual story reading where evidence, source and verification remain recoverable.

Pressure:
- larger `section`/`pause` than admin surfaces;
- `body`/`display` tuned for sustained reading;
- provenance `inscription` without badge soup;
- expressive accent distinct from state semantics.

### Quem São Eles?
Needs sober profile reading, chronology and archived source visibility.

Pressure:
- chronology and metadata rhythm;
- source/archive inscription;
- local editorial identity preserved through theme overrides.

### Franklin blog / The Theater / Rationality Club LatAm / Adi blog
These publications deliberately range from personal essays to fiction, generated community diaries and a small personal blog.

Pressure:
- shared reading roles must work without one editorial skin;
- small publications must not require heavyweight configuration;
- chronology/archive roles should scale from simple blog history to episodic narrative;
- provenance requirements must remain contextual rather than universal journalism chrome.

## Authoring/preview tools

### Intuit
Needs source/editor and visual preview with clear pane/control ownership and responsive orientation.

Pressure:
- focus and selected states stronger than in passive reading;
- geometry/boundaries that clarify workspace regions;
- light/dark local aesthetic can override values without changing semantics.

### Formato Legal
Needs live authoring, A4 preview, theme switching, export and print.

Pressure:
- authoring controls and rendered document need distinct but related type/spacing roles;
- multiple radically different themes must preserve semantic roles;
- print/document projection must not require a second foundation ontology.

## Cross-context checks

A candidate role fails if:
- it requires one fixed density for CausaGanha, Astronauta and O Vigia;
- a brand color must also mean success/warning to keep a theme coherent;
- publication typography roles cannot map to formal document/print output;
- editor/preview surfaces require bespoke foundation names that have no semantic distinction;
- large analytical tables need a parallel state vocabulary;
- a small blog needs to import a large component/runtime layer to receive the foundation.

## Promotion rule

A role may begin experimental from one consumer. Promotion toward stable core should prefer evidence across at least two distinct consumer contexts or a strong platform/accessibility invariant.

If a role exists only because one product has a specific workflow, legal convention or brand need, keep it local and link it from the consumer registry instead of expanding core.
